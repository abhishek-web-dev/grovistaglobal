COMPANY OVERVIEW

Driving Growth,
Delivering Excellence

Grovista Global Pvt Ltd, incorporated on 24 July 2025, is a fast-growing business solutions and support services company headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. The company is led by seasoned industry experts Mr. Sanjay Razdan and Ms. Jyoti Razdan, bringing decades of experience in business development, operations, HR, training, revenue enhancement, and end-to-end service delivery.

As a multi-vertical service provider, Grovista Global specializes in offering comprehensive consulting, outsourcing, operational management, and end-to-end support services, designed to power business growth, efficiency, and profitability.

With a strong commitment to excellence, accountability, and growth-driven partnerships, Grovista Global aims to become a trusted partner for organizations looking to scale sustainably and operate with precision.
